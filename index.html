Comic reader for Android

Gutter

Reads the comics you already own, straight from your phone's folders.

Coming to Google Play
  • FormatsCBZ, CBR (RAR4 and RAR5) and PDF.
  • Your libraryPick the folders your comics live in. Gutter builds a shelf with covers and remembers your page in every book.
  • OfflineReading never touches the network. No account to make.
  • No adsNone. Reading is free. One optional purchase adds library backup and restore.
  • Bring your ownGutter doesn't come with comics. It reads the files you already have.
